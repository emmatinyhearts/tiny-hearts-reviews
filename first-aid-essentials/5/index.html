Reply from Tiny HeartsTiny Hearts replied

Hi Sarah, Firstly, thank you for supporting Tiny Hearts, I am sad that this product hasn't met your expectations but I am positive we can assist you with some tips on how to position to get reliable readings; I have sent you an email. Just also wanted to note it's normal to get different readings in each ear, in order to track a temperature correctly you want to use the same ear each reading, rather than switching ears. Our thermometers are tested to be within +0.3 degrees celsius. The way infrared technology works is the sensor will collect infrared energy emitted by the ear drum, which is why the position of the probe is imperative. Depending on age, you might pull the ear straight back or back and upwards at the same time. We look forward to hearing from you so we can help you achieve accurate and reliable readings each time. Kindest, Tiny Hearts
