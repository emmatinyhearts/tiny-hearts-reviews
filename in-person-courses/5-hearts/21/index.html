I cannot thank Tiny Hearts enough for the wealth of knowledge and confidence I’ve received through their first aid course! This is the second time I’ve done their course and I cannot recommend it enough for any parent, parent to be or caregiver. It was absolutely amazing to see Rach and hear all her experiences and receive the tools to confidently provide first aid on an infant or child (Rach your moves to DRSABCD will forever be in my mind!). I was fortunate enough to stumble across Tiny Hearts 5 years ago and have relied on them to give me the confidence to help my 3 kids through the most stressful times. They are the reason I have been able to confidently assess my child’s health and provide the necessary first aid or escalate to 000 when needed. My first aid knowledge is as a result of their dedication to helping the wider community and I will always be grateful for the opportunity to have access to their course and their amazing products like fever buddy, medication tracker and now the newly released solids course. The Tiny Hearts first aid course is a no brainer for anyone with an infant or child within their care, it is an absolute must!
