Who is behind Tiny Hearts Education?

Tiny Hearts Education was founded by sisters Nikki and Rach. As a former paramedic, Nikki was often called to emergencies where confident first aid could have changed the outcome, so together they created the baby first aid course to help parents feel confident, educated and empowered to act. The team now teaches baby and child first aid to parents and carers across Australia, in person and online.
