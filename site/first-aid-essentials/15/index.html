First Aid Kit

Overall, I was very impressed with the kit. Please note that the below items are minor, and wouldn’t change my mind about purchasing again. Just a couple of things that I didn’t expect. I had thought that the kit would be secured with a latch to make it more challenging for the child prying fingers to access the kit. Also, the font of the text on the individual kit items can make it difficult to read what it is, quickly and fast. I imagine in an event, it may be a little frustrating for the flustered novice (that I am) to figure out which one I am looking for quickly. Again, I am impressed with the overall item and definitely agree that it is one of the best fitted out as well as good looking kits on the market.
